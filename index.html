Track FAQ & Visitor Quick Reference

Where is Swamp Bottom Dragstrip located?

Swamp Bottom Dragstrip is situated on 300+ acres in Columbia, Marion County, Mississippi, conveniently accessible from the US-98 and MS-13 transit corridors.

What is the track surface and length?

The facility features a full-length quarter-mile (1/4-mile) poured concrete racing surface equipped with digital timing equipment, extensive shutdown runout, and return lanes.

What events and classes run at the facility?

As an official member of the NHRA Division 4 South Central network, programs include Summit Racing Equipment E.T. Series, Jr. Dragster League, Street Legal test sessions, and bracket racing series.
